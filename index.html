RVK Studios was formed in 2012 by Baltasar Kormákur, the director behind 101 Reykjavík, Everest and Apex. His long-held plan: a film village in Reykjavík with stages built to the same standard as any studio in the world. Today Gufunes holds three sound stages, 6,500 square metres of studio floor and a growing cluster of rental, VFX and post companies. Productions shoot on the stages, then step outside into the landscape that brought them here. The stages carry the production. The country plays every part.
